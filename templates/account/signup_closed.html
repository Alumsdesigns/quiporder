Account Registration

Administrator Access Required

For security reasons, all Quiporder accounts are created by authorized administrators only. This ensures proper verification of healthcare professionals and maintains patient data security.

For New Employees:

  • Contact your department administrator or HR
  • Provide your employee ID and department details
  • Your account will be created within 24 hours
  • You'll receive login credentials via secure email

For Administrators:

To create a new user account ask HR for admin credentials and once recieved:

  1. Login to the admin panel
  2. Navigate to Users → Add user
  3. Fill in employee details and assign appropriate role
  4. Provide credentials to the new user securely
Security Note: This restriction protects patient data and ensures compliance by limiting system access to verified healthcare professionals only.
